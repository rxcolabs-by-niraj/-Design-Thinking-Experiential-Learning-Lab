About the program

Textbooks teach answers.
The real world asks questions.

Our programs close the gap between what classrooms teach and what careers, startups and organisations actually demand.

✕ The learning gap

Why traditional academic routes fall short

Students and early-career professionals master technical textbooks, formulas and domain theory — yet are rarely taught how to spot ambiguous real-world problems, empathise with users, or test solutions in the field.

  • ✕No structured design thinking frameworks
  • ✕No exposure to real-world user & market discovery
  • ✕Underdeveloped pitching & storytelling confidence
  • ✕Passive memorisation instead of team experimentation
Rapid industry evolution: healthcare, digital enterprises and modern startups now demand creative problem-solvers who understand desirability, feasibility and viability — not just technical operators.
✓ Our solution

Experiential learning beyond the classroom

We combine Design Thinking, Systems Thinking and MIT's Disciplined Entrepreneurship principles into an energetic, activity-based sprint where participants learn by doing.

  • 01

    Learn by solving real challenges

    Cross-functional teams dissect real healthcare and business problems, build tangible prototypes and test them with peers.

  • 02

    Facilitated by a startup practitioner

    Guided by real venture experience — from clinical hospital pilots and patent filings to 65+ college and corporate workshops.

  • 03

    Tailored to your audience

    Adaptable for student cohorts, faculty, MSMEs, entrepreneurs and corporate teams — with 1-Day and 2-Day formats.
